# 05 — Service Catalog

## Principle
Services and fields are configuration data, not hard-coded application forms. An administrator must be able to add/edit/deactivate services and configure their fields without source-code changes.

## Service ownership
Each service belongs to a requester department for V1. Initial departments: Fiscal, DP and Contábil.

**Demais Departamentos** (added 2026-10-19) is a GC-only requester department (not a `public.dpto` row): members of any corporate department that is not registered as a requester department may open requests in it. Requesters still see only their own requests. It has a single service, "Outros", and no correction service.

## "Outros" (added 2026-10-19)
Service "Outros" in Fiscal, DP, Contábil and Demais Departamentos — Nome da empresa (optional); Código da empresa (optional); Descrição da solicitação (required); Observações. The company picker fills the company fields.

## Request number is automatic
"Número do chamado" in Senha gerente (Fiscal, DP) and Liberação/cadastro de empresa (Contábil) is the request's own number (protocolo `GC-YYYY-NNNNNN`), generated by the system on submission. The requester does not fill it in: those seeded fields are deactivated (decided 2026-10-14). "Número do chamado do Onvio" is an external ticket reference and stays a requester field.

## Common field
Every service exposes `Observações` as a free-text field in addition to service-specific fields. It is available but not mandatory unless later configured.

## Correction/rework service
Each requester department must have `Correção de Cadastro / Refazer` with at least: Nome da empresa, Código da empresa, Serviço original que precisa de correção, referência à solicitação/OS anterior se houver, Motivo da correção and Observações.

Since Phase 6 the original request is a mandatory link chosen in the form (closed request of the same department), which already identifies the original service and protocol. The seeded fields "Serviço original que precisa de correção" (`servico_original`) and "Solicitação/OS anterior" (`solicitacao_anterior`) overlap with that link but are kept active for now (decided 2026-10-14); they can be deactivated later in the catalog without code changes.

## Open configuration questions
Before seed creation confirm field types, which fields are selects, allowed values for Tributação, Contabilidade, Impostos and Prestados/Tomados, and attachment constraints.
